Chat-Accounting is free and open source desktop accounting software for small businesses and freelancers. It runs locally on your computer and stores your company data in a single `.db` file, so you keep full control of your books.

Chat-Accounting builds on [Frappe Books](https://github.com/frappe/books), and this documentation is adapted from the [Frappe Books docs](https://docs.frappe.io/books) under AGPL-3.0. Each page records its upstream source in `sourceUrl` frontmatter.
